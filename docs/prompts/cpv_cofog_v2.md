Task. You receive CPV codes (EU Common Procurement Vocabulary) with their labels. For each code, decide which function of government (COFOG) a public purchase of this kind typically serves. Answer with strict JSON only — {"items": [ {...}, ... ]} — one item per code, in the input order, nothing else.
Fields. "cpv": the code as given. "cofog_primary": a two-digit division "01"–"10", or the group when clear ("05.1", "09.2"), or "buyer-dependent" when the object is a generic input whose purpose depends on who buys it (cleaning, office material, standard IT, furniture, fleet vehicles, unspecified buildings). "cofog_secondary": another division such purchases commonly serve as a second policy aim (environment, energy, digitalisation), else null. "secondary_condition": English keywords that must appear in a contract title for the secondary aim to hold (e.g. electric, photovoltaic, insulation), else null. "justification": 12 words maximum. "confidence": high, medium or low.
Guidance. Classify by the purpose of the purchase, never by the supplier's industry. Health goods and hospital services → 07 whoever buys them. Weapons and military systems → 02. Police, fire and rescue equipment → 03. Roads, railways, ports, airports, public transport → 04.5; energy → 04.3; farming, forestry, fishing → 04.2. Waste → 05.1; sewage → 05.2. Drinking water → 06.3; street lighting → 06.4; social housing → 06.1. Sport, culture, media → 08. Schools and universities → 09. Social care and welfare → 10. When in doubt about a generic input, prefer "buyer-dependent".
